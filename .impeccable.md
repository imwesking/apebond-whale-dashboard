## Design Context

### Users
Growth, product, and operations users monitor ApeBond wallet activity, bonded volume, partner performance, and buyer acquisition signals. They need fast readouts that support repeated analysis rather than a marketing-style landing experience.

### Brand Personality
Calm, analytical, and practical. The interface should feel trustworthy, compact, and source-led, with enough visual contrast to make wallet and volume movement easy to scan.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the existing light operational dashboard style: tinted neutral surfaces, restrained green and blue accents, tight tables, compact KPI strips, and chart-led panels. Avoid decorative hero layouts, heavy gradients, and overly playful styling.

### Design Principles
- Put the metric or task first on every page.
- Keep controls dense, predictable, and useful for repeat work.
- Make charts explain movement without hiding the underlying counts.
- Preserve responsive layouts so mobile users can still inspect the same data.
- Prefer source freshness and caveats near the numbers they affect.
